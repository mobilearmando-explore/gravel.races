Read this before planning

This is a curated planning calendar, not an official sanctioning calendar. Registration status, exact route surface, and sellout status can change quickly. Event links now point to official event or registration pages wherever verified; click through before booking travel.

Most national dates are drawn from Velo’s 2026 gravel racing calendar, with event links replaced by official event/registration pages where available. Texas-local additions come from the earlier Texas-focused registration/event research.
